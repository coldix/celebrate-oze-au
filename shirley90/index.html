The day

A celebration by the water

On 25 July 2026, family and friends gathered at Karbeethong Lodge in Mallacoota to celebrate Shirley Dixon’s 90th birthday. It was an afternoon of shared food, conversation, photographs and music — a warm, multi-generation gathering in one of East Gippsland’s most beautiful settings.

This page is the lasting record of that day: the story, media, messages and venue that made it special — and a working example of what Celebrate can create for a milestone.

Venue

Karbeethong Lodge

Karbeethong Lodge is a welcoming waterfront lodge in Mallacoota — well suited to family celebrations and group gatherings. For Shirley’s 90th it provided space to gather across generations, share food and enjoy the setting without feeling formal or commercial.

Celebrate is not the booking agent, owner or operator of the lodge. For accommodation, availability, pricing and full venue details, please contact the lodge directly or visit the official website.
